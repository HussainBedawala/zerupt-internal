# Value Equation Deep-Dive: Dream Outcome

> Source: $100M Offers, Chapter 9 — "The Value Equation" (Numerator, Variable 1)

## Core Principle

The Dream Outcome is NOT your product. It's not your features. It's not what you do. It's **the end state your customer is trying to reach.**

People don't buy drills — they buy holes. They don't buy holes — they buy the shelf on the wall. They don't buy the shelf — they buy the organized home. They don't buy the organized home — they buy the feeling of control and pride.

**Go as deep as you can into what they ACTUALLY want.**

## How to Identify the Dream Outcome

### The "So that..." Chain
Keep asking "so that..." until you hit the emotional core:
- "I want more leads" → so that... "I get more customers" → so that... "I make more revenue" → so that... "I can hire and stop doing everything myself" → so that... "I have freedom and my business runs without me"

The LAST answer is the real dream outcome.

### The Gap
- **Where they are now** = Current State (pain, frustration, limitation)
- **Where they want to be** = Dream Outcome (desire, relief, transformation)
- **Your offer bridges this gap**

## Dream Outcome Categories

Hormozi identifies that dream outcomes usually fall into these buckets:
1. **Make money** (increase revenue, profit, income)
2. **Save money** (reduce costs, eliminate waste)
3. **Save time** (automate, delegate, streamline)
4. **Reduce effort** (make it easier, remove complexity)
5. **Increase status** (look good, feel important, gain respect)
6. **Improve health/wellbeing** (reduce stress, improve quality of life)

## Making the Dream Outcome Bigger

The BIGGER the dream outcome, the more valuable the offer:
- Not "get more leads" → "fill your pipeline so you never worry about revenue again"
- Not "save time on accounting" → "get your nights and weekends back"
- Not "better inventory management" → "never lose a sale to stockouts again"

Make it vivid. Make it emotional. Make it specific.

## Interview Questions for This Phase

1. What is the #1 result your customer wants? (Not your product — their end state)
2. If your product worked PERFECTLY, what would their life/business look like?
3. What are they most frustrated about RIGHT NOW? (Current state)
4. What would they pay almost anything to achieve?
5. Complete: "My customer's dream is to _____ so that they can _____"
