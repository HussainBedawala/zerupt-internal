## What Zerupt is (one breath)

The world's first agentic AI retail **business brain**. A retail owner signs up and is
**up and running on their real data in under 2 hours** — no consultants, no implementation project.
Built for MENA first (proper Arabic, GCC VAT), then Southeast Asia and India. Solo
founder: Hussain. Launching June 15, 2026 at zerupt.com.

## Positioning rules (hard)

- **Lead emotional, not category:** "Your business, handled. Know your next move." Category
  words (POS, accounting, inventory, ZATCA) live one layer down for comprehension + SEO.
- **Never lead a customer surface with "ERP"** — but BE the ERP in metadata and comparison
  pages so intent-searchers find us.
- **Lead with the wedge (painkiller), not the AI vision (expansion):**
  "Switch off your slow billing software that can't handle Arabic properly — in one afternoon. Fast Arabic invoicing,
  your barcode and invoice layout your way, compliance-ready, and we move all your old
  data for free."
- The agentic "Your Next Move" AI advisor is the UPSELL/expansion story, not the hook.
- **No em dashes or en dashes in any published copy** (standing founder rule).

## Who we sell to (ICP)

GCC retail owners, 1-5 outlets, ~$300K-10M/yr revenue, currently on spreadsheets, a basic
POS, or legacy desktop software (Tally, Vyapar, Zoho, Merpec-class systems). Their pains:
broken Arabic, slow checkout, no invoice/barcode customization, VAT/ZATCA anxiety, and the
big switching blocker: **fear of losing old data on migration**. Our self-serve migration
import (with free done-for-you migration in the offer) kills that objection.

- **Primary ICP:** independent/SME owner-operator across MENA, India, and SEA.
- **GTM beachhead: GCC** (Kuwait, Saudi, UAE, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar). India/SEA = phase 2 in rollout — not excluded from the brand's audience.
- These users are NOT tech-savvy. Marketing must feel safe, simple, and concrete.

## Pricing & offer (locked 2026-06, v1 hypothesis pending prospect validation)

- **Per OUTLET, unlimited staff logins.** Never per-seat.
- Tiers (SAR/mo, 1 outlet): **Starter 149** (~$40, basic alerts only) ·
  **Growth 349 ⭐ Most Popular** (full "Your Next Move" AI advisor, 50 AI actions/mo) ·
  **Pro 699** (unlimited AI) · **Chains** (custom). Extra outlet +99/+149/+249.
  KWD anchors: 12/29/55. Annual = 2 months free. Billed in local currency.
- Core ERP + ZATCA compliance are NEVER gated; only scale + AI depth are gated.
- **Guarantee (2 parts):** (1) up and running in under 2 hours or we do setup + migration free and
  you don't pay until you're running; (2) 30-day clarity-or-full-refund.
- **Founding 50:** lifetime-locked founder pricing, public live seat counter for urgency.
- GTM motion: no card at signup → concierge onboarding = activation → card after they're up and running.
  Reverse trial, never permanent freemium.

## What is actually BUILT (safe to claim) vs planned

Always verify with `get_codemap` / `list_modules` before making capability claims. As of
2026-06-07, built and working: full double-entry accounting (GCC COAs, VAT), inventory
(WAC costing, transfers, barcodes, label/receipt printing), POS (offline mode, returns,
store credit), sales, purchase, onboarding wizard, self-serve imports (COA, items,
customers, suppliers, trial balance, opening stock, AR/AP per-invoice aging), reports,
multi-currency, Arabic/English RTL throughout. AI: invoice scanner + migration brain are
IN ACTIVE BUILD for the June 15 MVP — check codemaps/specs before promising specifics.
NEVER promise a spec-only feature as available today.

## Brand voice & visual identity

- Voice: confident, warm, concrete, zero enterprise jargon. Speak to a shop owner, not a
  CFO. Arabic-first respect (never machine-translated-feeling copy).
- Visual: warm cream `#F9F7F5` canvas, ink `#141310`, citron `#979C1A` accent (fill only,
  never text on light), olive `#747818`/`#454729` + sage `#ADAB92` for data viz. IBM Plex
  Sans (Latin+Arabic+Devanagari) + IBM Plex Mono. Premium, editorial, dark-first. Never
  violet/teal, never Inter/Noto.
- **Brand authority (canonical):** `agent-os/brand/brand-foundation.md` (MCP resource: `brand`).

## Never say / always say

- Never: "ERP" as the hook, per-user pricing, "free forever", uptime/SLA promises,
  features not in codemaps, India pricing, fundraising/VC framing (bootstrapped), "ZATCA-ready" (not yet shipped).
- Always: up and running in under 2 hours, free migration, proper Arabic, GCC VAT compliance, unlimited staff
  logins, Founding 50 scarcity (with the real seat number), the two-part guarantee. For KSA: label ZATCA e-invoicing as "coming soon".
