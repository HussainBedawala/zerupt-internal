## TL;DR

Zerupt is the world's first agentic AI retail business brain. It gives independent retail owner-operators in MENA, India, and SEA one place to run their whole business — and tells them their next move to grow it. Not software you operate. A business brain that works for you.

---

## Mission

Build the smartest, most beautiful, most trustworthy system a retail owner has ever touched — one that does the work for them, speaks their language, and actively points them toward their next dollar of growth.

**Founding constraint:** Zerupt must never cost more time than it saves. Every feature, every screen, every word is measured against this.

---

## Who It's For

**Primary ICP:** the independent / SME retail owner-operator in MENA, India, and Southeast Asia.

- Archetype: Mohammed (GCC) / Pacific Co. owner (SEA) — smart about business, not software
- Time-starved, hands-on, often runs the shop themselves
- May operate in Arabic, Hindi, or English
- Today: overwhelmed, firefighting, blind to their real numbers, wants to grow but doesn't know how

**NOT:** mid-market enterprise. NOT the NetSuite / SAP segment. NOT someone with an IT team.

**Markets:** MENA first (GCC launch), then SEA + India. Launch: June 15, 2026.

---

## What It Does — 3 Pillars

1. **One place for everything** — sales, stock, accounting, operations, all unified. No more spreadsheets, no more fragmented apps, no more missing the picture.
2. **It works for you** — done-for-you operations. The system runs the busywork so the owner manages, not operates. No manual data entry. No fighting the tool.
3. **It grows you** — a directive advisor. Zerupt tells the owner their next move: what to stock, what's running out, where money is leaking, what to do this week. Not a dashboard to decode — insight handed to you.

**The signature moment:** a recurring, citron-marked UI surface where Zerupt says what to do next — one clear recommendation, one tap to act, the why on demand. This is the brand's soul made interactive.

- **Primary tagline:** "Your business, handled."
- **Sub-line / growth advisor:** "Know your next move."
- **Boilerplate:** Zerupt is the world's first agentic AI retail business brain — one premium place for retail owners across MENA, India, and Southeast Asia to run everything and grow faster, without the busywork.

**Category:** the retail business brain that runs your shop and tells you how to grow.
**Enemy:** software that makes you work for it — legacy ERP bloat, manual chaos, operate-it-yourself tools.

## Why It Wins

- **Signup to live in under 2 hours.** AI handles configuration, data import, and setup. No implementation partner. No consultant. No months-long project.
- **Agentic, not just automated.** The system watches, advises, and acts — not just stores data and waits for queries.
- **Built for the owner, not the accountant.** Every surface is designed for a non-technical, time-starved operator. Radical simplicity over feature completeness.
- **Language-first.** Arabic RTL from day one. IBM Plex Sans covers Latin, Arabic, and Devanagari in one family. MENA/India/SEA tax compliance baked in — not bolted on.
- **10 years of real retail operations.** Every workflow and edge case comes from production use across real retail businesses. No generic AI guessing — domain patterns are learned.
