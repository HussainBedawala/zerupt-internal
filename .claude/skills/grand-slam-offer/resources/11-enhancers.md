# Phase 8: Offer Enhancers — Scarcity, Urgency, Bonuses, Guarantees

> Source: $100M Offers, Chapters 13-14 — "Enhancing the Offer"

## Core Principle

Once you have your core offer stack, you ENHANCE it with four psychological levers that increase urgency to buy and reduce perceived risk. These are not tricks — they are genuine value enhancers when done authentically.

---

## 1. Scarcity (Limited Quantity)

**"Only X spots available"**

Scarcity works because people value what's rare. But it MUST be genuine.

### Types of Genuine Scarcity
- **Capacity scarcity**: You can only serve X clients per month (real operational limit)
- **Cohort scarcity**: Only X spots per cohort/batch
- **Bonus scarcity**: "First X buyers get [extra thing]"
- **Tier scarcity**: "Only X spots at this price tier"

### Rules
- NEVER fake scarcity — it destroys trust permanently
- Tie scarcity to a real constraint (your time, team capacity, server limits, etc.)
- Explain WHY it's limited (makes it believable)

---

## 2. Urgency (Limited Time)

**"Available until [date]"**

Urgency compresses the decision timeline. Again — must be genuine.

### Types of Genuine Urgency
- **Cohort-based**: "Next cohort starts [date], enrollment closes [date]"
- **Seasonal**: "Launch pricing ends [date]"
- **Event-driven**: "This offer is tied to [event/milestone]"
- **Rolling deadline**: "You have 48 hours from when you see this"
- **Price increase**: "Price goes up on [date]" (and it actually does)

### Rules
- If you say the deadline is Tuesday, the offer MUST disappear on Tuesday
- Evergreen urgency (fake countdown timers) = dishonest and harmful
- Explain WHY the deadline exists

---

## 3. Bonuses (Added Value)

**"You also get..."**

Bonuses increase the total perceived value without changing the price. They make the gap between price and value even wider.

### Effective Bonus Strategy
1. **Each bonus solves a related but different problem** — not the same problem your core offer solves
2. **Each bonus has its own value anchor** — "This alone is worth $X because..."
3. **Bonuses should be high-value, low-cost to deliver** — digital assets, templates, recorded trainings, tools
4. **Stack bonuses** — 3-5 bonuses is the sweet spot. More than that feels gimmicky.
5. **Name each bonus** — give it a specific name that communicates the value

### Bonus Value Anchoring
For each bonus, state:
- What it is
- What problem it solves
- What it's worth on its own (and why)
- Why you're including it

---

## 4. Guarantees (Risk Reversal)

**"If [condition], then [what you'll do]"**

Guarantees transfer risk from the buyer to you. The stronger the guarantee, the more confidence the buyer has.

### Types of Guarantees (from Hormozi)

#### Unconditional Guarantee
- "If you don't like it for any reason, full refund. No questions asked."
- Strongest risk reversal
- Works when: you have high confidence in your product AND your market isn't full of serial refunders

#### Conditional Guarantee
- "If you do X, Y, and Z and don't get [result], we'll [refund / work for free / etc.]"
- Ties the guarantee to the customer taking action
- Works when: the result depends on customer effort
- Example: "If you implement all 5 modules, attend all calls, and don't add 10 new customers in 90 days, we'll refund 100%"

#### Anti-Guarantee
- "All sales are final. This is NOT for everyone."
- Creates exclusivity and filters for serious buyers
- Works when: your reputation and proof are so strong that demanding commitment INCREASES perceived value
- Hormozi notes: this can work well for premium, high-ticket offers

#### Implied Guarantee
- No explicit guarantee stated, but the risk is low enough naturally
- Example: month-to-month SaaS with easy cancellation = implied guarantee
- Works when: the barrier to exit is already low

### Choosing the Right Guarantee
- **New business, little proof?** → Strong unconditional guarantee (you need to absorb more risk to earn trust)
- **Established, lots of proof?** → Conditional guarantee or anti-guarantee
- **SaaS with free trial?** → The trial IS your guarantee
- **High-ticket?** → Conditional guarantee with specific deliverables

---

## Interview Goal

By the end of this phase, you should have:
- A scarcity mechanism that's genuine and explained
- An urgency mechanism tied to a real constraint
- 3-5 bonuses, each named, valued, and solving a specific adjacent problem
- A guarantee type chosen with clear terms
