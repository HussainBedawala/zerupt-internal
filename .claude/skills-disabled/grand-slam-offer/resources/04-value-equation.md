# Phase 4: The Value Equation

> Source: $100M Offers, Chapter 9 — "The Value Equation"

## The Equation

```
                Dream Outcome  x  Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value = ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                    Time Delay  x  Effort & Sacrifice
```

This is the CORE framework of the entire book. Every decision about your offer should map back to this equation.

**To increase value:**
- MAXIMIZE the numerator (Dream Outcome, Perceived Likelihood)
- MINIMIZE the denominator (Time Delay, Effort & Sacrifice)

## The 4 Variables

### 1. Dream Outcome (Maximize)
- What is the #1 result your customer wants?
- Not your product features — their END STATE
- The bigger and more desirable the dream outcome, the more valuable the offer
- Think: what would they pay anything for?

### 2. Perceived Likelihood of Achievement (Maximize)
- Does the customer BELIEVE they will actually get the result?
- Belief drivers: social proof, case studies, guarantees, credentials, specificity, track record
- The more certain they are it will work, the more they'll pay
- This is why testimonials, data, and guarantees matter so much

### 3. Time Delay (Minimize)
- How long between purchase and result?
- Shorter = dramatically more valuable
- People pay premiums for speed
- Can you deliver quick wins early while working toward the big result?
- "Immediate" beats "eventually" every time

### 4. Effort & Sacrifice (Minimize)
- What does the customer have to DO or GIVE UP?
- Less effort = more valuable
- The hierarchy: Done-For-You > Done-With-You > Do-It-Yourself
- What can you take OFF their plate?
- What sacrifices can you eliminate?

## How to Use the Equation

For every element of your offer, ask:
1. Does this increase the dream outcome or perceived likelihood? → KEEP/ENHANCE
2. Does this reduce time delay or effort? → KEEP/ENHANCE
3. Does this do neither? → CUT IT
4. Does this accidentally INCREASE time or effort? → REDESIGN IT

## Interview Goal

By the end of this phase, you should have:
- A crystal-clear dream outcome statement
- An honest assessment of current perceived likelihood (and how to increase it)
- The current time-to-result and ideas to shorten it
- The current effort/sacrifice required and ideas to reduce it
- A value equation "scorecard" for the current vs. improved offer
