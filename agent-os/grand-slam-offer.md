## 8. Enhancers

### Scarcity
- **Mechanism:** "Founding 100" — first 100 customers get locked-in Founder pricing forever ($39/user/month, no annual commitment required). After 100 customers, price goes to $49/user/month minimum.
- **Why it's genuine:** Solo founder, limited support bandwidth. First 100 customers will shape the product — their feedback is worth the discount. After 100, the feedback loop is established and the price reflects the proven product.
- **How to communicate:** "I'm building Zerupt with my first 100 customers. You get lifetime Founder pricing. In exchange, I ask for honest feedback and the occasional 15-minute call. Once we hit 100, the door closes."

### Urgency
- **Mechanism:** Founding 100 spots are filled on a rolling basis. Public counter on the website showing remaining spots. When a slot fills, it's gone.
- **Why it's genuine:** There are literally only 100 spots at this tier. The counter is real.
- **How to communicate:** "73 of 100 Founding Member spots remaining. Lock in $39/user/month for life."

### Guarantee
- **Type:** Implied + Conditional hybrid
- **Terms:**
  - **Implied:** Month-to-month billing. Cancel anytime. Export your data anytime. No lock-in. No exit fees. (The SaaS model IS the guarantee.)
  - **Conditional:** "Complete the 2-Hour Launch onboarding and import your data. If you don't have real-time visibility into your business within 24 hours of going live, we'll personally hop on a call, fix whatever's wrong, and give you the next 3 months free."
- **Why this type:** New business with little social proof needs to absorb risk. But the conditional element ensures only serious buyers qualify — they have to actually complete onboarding. The "3 months free" is more believable than "full refund" for SaaS (refund implies failure; free months implies confidence).
