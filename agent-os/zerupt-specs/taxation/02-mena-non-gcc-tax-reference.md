## Egypt — Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Tax type | VAT |
| Standard rate | 14% |
| Zero-rated | Exports |
| Exempt | Banking, medicine, healthcare, agricultural products |
| Inclusive/Exclusive | Exclusive |
| Filing frequency | Monthly |
| E-invoicing | **Mandatory — ETA platform (B2B, B2C, B2G)** |

### E-Invoicing
- Fully mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses
- B2B e-invoicing: fully rolled out
- B2C e-receipts: phased rollout, 8th sub-phase started Sep 2025
- Format: XML or JSON with electronic signature
- ETA validates invoice before it can be issued to customer
- Required fields: customer details, tax ID, UUID, electronic signature

- [ClearTax — E-invoicing Egypt](https://www.cleartax.com/eg/en/e-invoicing-egypt)

## Iraq

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Tax type | **Selective sales tax** (no general VAT) |
| Standard rate | No general VAT |
| Selective rates | See below |
| E-invoicing | Not applicable |

### Selective Sales Tax Rates
| Category | Rate |
|----------|------|
| Alcohol and tobacco (cigarettes) | 300% |
| Travel tickets | 15% |
| Cars | 15% |
| Mobile recharge cards and internet | 20% |
| Deluxe/first-class restaurants and hotels | 10% |

### Notes
- VAT implementation has been discussed but not enacted
- **For Zerupt: treat as selective excise taxes, not general consumption tax**
- Most retail transactions have no sales tax

## Morocco — Direction Generale des Impots (DGI)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Tax type | VAT |
| Standard rate | 20% |
| Reduced rates | 10%, 7% (transitional — simplifying to 0%, 10%, 20% by end 2026) |
| Inclusive/Exclusive | Exclusive |
| Filing frequency | Monthly (turnover > MAD 1M), quarterly (others) |
| E-invoicing | Not mandatory yet |

### Rate Details (transitional period 2024-2026)
| Rate | Applies to |
|------|-----------|
| 20% | Standard rate — most goods and services |
| 10% | Hotels, restaurants, petroleum, financial services |
| 7% | Water, sugar, medicines (being phased into 0% or 10%) |

### 2024 Finance Act Reform
- Simplifying to three tiers: **0%, 10%, 20%** by end of 2026
- 7% and 14% rates being phased out or merged
