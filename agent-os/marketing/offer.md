## 0. What changed from the March offer (and why)

| March 2026 (discarded) | June 2026 (this doc) | Why |
| -- | -- | -- |
| **Per-user** pricing ($49/user/mo) | **Per-outlet** pricing | Retail staff are high-churn cashiers; per-seat makes owners share logins and *fights our own agentic product* (AI reduces seats). Per-outlet maps to how owners think about growth → clean expansion revenue. |

(Neon usage-based + scale-to-zero, shared Railway/Vercel, cached daily AI advisor). At any price
here we run **95%+ gross margin**. Pricing is set 100% by *value*, never cost.

## 1. Market: the starving crowd

**WHO:** Retail owners with 1 to 5 outlets in the **GCC** (Kuwait, Saudi, UAE, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar),
doing roughly $300K to $10M/yr, running on spreadsheets, a basic POS, or a legacy desktop system.

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Massive pain | 9 | Can't see real-time profit/stock/cash; VAT/ZATCA anxiety every period; every "real" ERP needs consultants + months. |
| Purchasing power | 9 | GCC owners already pay SAR 100 to 600/mo across cobbled tools (POS + accounting + bookkeeper). High WTP, VAT mandates *force* software adoption. |
| Easy to target | 7 | Intent SEO ("POS software Kuwait/KSA/UAE"), retail WhatsApp/Instagram groups, partner-led, founder content. |
| Growing | 9 | Vision 2030 SMB digitization; ZATCA e-invoicing is a regulatory tailwind. |

**Verdict:** pain + money + regulatory push. The implementation barrier is the dam;
"up and running in 2 hours" breaks it.

## 3. Dream outcome & the "so that" chain

**Current state:** *"I close at 10pm, open Excel, and still can't tell if I made money. I have 3
shops and don't know which is bleeding. I reorder by gut. VAT filing terrifies me. SAP wanted $50K;
Odoo needed a 4-month consultant. I gave up and went back to Excel."*

**Dream state:** *"I open my phone at breakfast and see exactly what each shop made yesterday, my
cash, what's running low, what's dead. My VAT return builds itself. New branch is ready in minutes.
I just signed up and it worked."*

**So-that chain:** real-time numbers → know which shops are profitable → decide on data not gut →
stop losing money blindly → **feel in control of my business instead of it controlling me.**

## 4. Value equation

`Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort)`

| Variable | Score | Lever |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Dream outcome | 9/10 | Real-time visibility + GCC VAT compliance (ZATCA e-invoicing coming soon) + multi-branch control + the "next move" advisor. |
| **Perceived likelihood** | **4 to 8 (the bottleneck)** | Nobody believes "2 hours". Every ERP lied before. Fix with **proof**: recorded real onboardings, live demo, the speed guarantee, founding-customer testimonials. |
| Time delay | 10/10 | **Under 2 hours** vs 6 to 18 months. Show their real P&L inside the first 30 min of onboarding. |
| Effort | 8→9 | AI self-config + AI data import + Arabic-first + concierge onboarding for the founding cohort. |

**The entire go-to-market job is raising *perceived likelihood*.** The dream is already huge and the
time delay is already world-class.

## 8. Enhancers: scarcity, urgency, guarantee

### Scarcity: Founding 50

First **50 GCC customers** lock **founder pricing for life** (frozen even as standard prices rise
post-launch). In exchange: honest feedback + a testimonial/case study + the occasional 15-min call.
**Genuine** because a solo founder can only give 50 customers real concierge attention. Kept at 50
(not 100) so it stays credible and high-touch.

### Urgency

Public counter: *"37 of 50 Founding seats left. Lock founder pricing for life."* Real, because there
are literally 50.

### Guarantee: two clean parts, both built on the 2-hour USP

**Part 1: The 2-hour setup guarantee (speed)**
> "We'll get you fully up and running (your real data imported, your real numbers on screen) in one
> onboarding session **under 2 hours**. If we can't, **we do the entire setup and migration for you,
> ourselves, free**, until you're up and running. You don't pay until you're running."

**Part 2: The 30-Day Clarity Guarantee (results)**
> "Run Zerupt for 30 days. If you don't have clearer visibility into your profit, stock, and cash
> than you've ever had (and you tell us why), we refund every fil. No lock-in, export your data
> anytime."

**Why this pair:** Part 1 attacks *time-delay* and *"too good to be true"* at decision. Part 2 attacks
*perceived likelihood* over the trial. Conditional on completing onboarding, so only serious buyers
qualify.

## 9. Go-to-market motion (how the offer is delivered)

Concierge + **reverse trial**, not self-serve card-required trial:

1. **No card at signup.** Lower the entry barrier.
2. **Concierge onboarding: the activation event.** Founder (or AI) gets them live with real data
   in the 2-hour session, peak trust.
3. **Capture card-on-file during onboarding**, right after they're live (card-after-value converts
   ~5× card-before-value).
4. After the trial → drops to the paid tier they chose, **never permanent freemium**.
