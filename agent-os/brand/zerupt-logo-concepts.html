Honest caveats vs. the simpler z. icon: (1) two stacked triangles + dot is busy at 16–32px — the dot and the smaller triangle muddy together, so it needs a simplified small-size variant. (2) Using citron + olive is two tones, which softly bends the "one accent" rule (olive is a system derivative, so it's defensible for a logo, but watch it). (3) The triangles read as a paper-plane / "send" to some eyes — worth a gut-check. Its big win: it's a real ownable symbol, where z. is just a letter.

Construction & clearspace

zerupt.

Clearspace on all sides = the x-height of the wordmark (the height of the "z"). Nothing — type, edges, other logos — intrudes into the shaded zone.
Dot: diameter ≈ 1.1× the letter stem width; sits on the baseline with a gap of half its diameter after the "t". Optically aligned, not mechanically.
Tracking: −3% across the wordmark. Weight: SemiBold (600). Case: always lowercase.
