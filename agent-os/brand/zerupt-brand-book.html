02Strategy

Purpose

To give every retail business owner the superpower of a world-class operations team, a wise advisor, and a premium back office — all in one place, at their fingertips — so they can stop drowning in work they shouldn't have to do and start growing the business they dreamed of.

Founding constraint: Zerupt must never cost more time than it saves. Every feature, screen, and word is measured against this.

Vision

A world where running a retail business no longer requires you to be chained to the counter, fluent in software, or able to afford an enterprise team. One place. One brain. Every owner, anywhere — operating like the best in the world.

Mission

Build the smartest, most beautiful, most trustworthy system a retail owner has ever touched — one that does the work for them, speaks their language, and actively points them toward their next dollar of growth.

Values

Obsessive quality

Accuracy and craft are sacred — especially with money and data. Never "good enough."

Radical simplicity

Power hidden behind effortlessness. If it's hard to use, we failed — not the user.

Customer-shaped

We bend to the customer's reality — their language, market, tax, habits. Never the reverse.

Earned trust

We guard their money, data, and livelihood as our own. Reliability is the price of entry.

03The Enemy & Positioning

The enemy — software that makes you work for it

  • Legacy ERP bloat — expensive, ugly, months to implement, built for accountants not owners.
  • The manual chaos — spreadsheets, notebooks, WhatsApp orders, gut-feel stock.
  • The operate-it-yourself paradigm — dumb tools that force humans to do data entry. You should manage, not operate.
We are not against non-English / local markets — we embrace them. The enemy is friction and busywork, never the customer's context.

The repositioning move

We do not lead with "ERP" (baggage: expensive, hard, slow) or the literal phrase "AI employee" (too abstract). Like the candle that sells the experience of time rather than "a scented candle," Zerupt sells the feeling of your whole business suddenly being handled — and knowing your next move.

Point of difference

Zerupt doesn't just store your business — it has a point of view about it. It does the work and tells you what to do next.

04Audience & Insight

Part B — Verbal Identity

06Voice Principles

Confident & bold. Short, declarative, a little swagger — but earned, never hype. We state outcomes as facts. We never use jargon. We speak in the language of the owner's life: time, money, peace, growth.

Spectrum: Confident not arrogant · Warm not soft · Premium not fancy · Direct not blunt · Calm not boring.

  1. Lead with the outcome, not the feature.
  2. Short sentences win — cut every word that isn't earning its place.
  3. Speak to one owner, never "users" or "businesses."
  4. No jargon, ever, in customer-facing copy.
  5. End with momentum — a next step, a result, a reason to act.

✓ Do

  • "Run your whole shop. From your phone. Done."
  • "Zerupt already knows what's selling. Here's what to stock next."
  • "You focus on the business. We handle the rest."

✕ Don't

  • "Our comprehensive ERP solution streamlines operations." (jargon, soft)
  • "You can configure inventory modules." (makes them work)
  • "Try our AI-powered platform today!" (generic hype)

08Lexicon & Naming

✓ Use

  • run, handle, grow, your next move, in one place, for you
  • business, shop, owner, money, time, stock
  • it knows, it tells you, it handles

✕ Avoid

  • ERP, module, SKU, configure, leverage, synergy, platform, solution
  • end-user, utilize, robust, seamless, "cutting-edge" as filler
  • dashboard (when "your numbers" works), onboard

Grammar: sentence case for UI and headlines (not Title Case). Numerals for quantities and money (IBM Plex Mono). Oxford comma. Contractions welcome. Never ALL-CAPS for emphasis — use weight.

Naming convention: name surfaces after the owner's outcome, not the engineering domain. "Your Numbers," not "Reporting Module."

Part C — Visual Identity

10Design Philosophy

Warm, editorial, restrained. A cream "paper" canvas, near-black ink, and a single earthy citron accent doing all the highlighting work. The opposite of generic SaaS violet. Premium and calm; the data is the hero, the chrome recedes. Dark-first with full light parity.

Three rules that override aesthetics

  1. One accent. Citron is the only brand color. No secondary hue competes.
  2. Citron never carries text on light. It's a fill / ring / data color only. Citron-toned text uses accent-text (olive-deep).
  3. Primary actions are ink, not citron. Citron marks focus, selection, active state, charts, and "Zerupt is acting."

11Logo

Two elements: the symbol (the rising-triangles "spark") and the wordmark (zerupt.). Used together in the horizontal lockup, or independently.

The wordmark & lockup

  • Wordmark: zerupt. — lowercase, IBM Plex Sans SemiBold (600), tracking −3%. Trailing dot is citron on primary versions only; mono lockups match the wordmark.
  • Lockup gap: symbol-to-wordmark gap = the wordmark's x-height.
  • Clearspace: = the x-height of the wordmark (height of the "z") on all sides.
  • Minimum size: wordmark 20px digital / 8mm print; symbol/icon 16px.
  • Variations: horizontal lockup · symbol only · wordmark only · z. app-icon · mono ink · mono cream.

✓ On imagery

  • Mono cream/ink only, on a calm area, with scrim if contrast < AA.

✕ Misuse

  • Citron wordmark on light · stretch/condense · rotate · shadow/glow · altered spacing · old violet palette · different typeface.
Production: wordmark renders in live IBM Plex Sans for review — outline to vector paths with tuned kerning before production.

14Grid & Layout

  • Spacing: 4px base; rhythm in multiples of 4 (8/16/24/32).
  • Columns: 12-column fluid grid; 24px gutter desktop, 16px mobile. Max content 1280px.
  • Breakpoints: sm 640 · md 768 · lg 1024 · xl 1280 · 2xl 1536px.
  • Radii: --radius-lg: 0.5rem (cards, buttons). Soft, not pill — pills reserved for the citron status/nav accent.
  • Elevation: subtle — 1px border + faint shadow. Dark mode uses surface lightness over shadow.
  • Print: A4, 18mm margins, 12-col grid, baseline to 11pt body leading.

15Iconography & Graphic Device

  • Icons: minimal line icons, 24px grid, 1.5px stroke, rounded joins. Quiet by default (ink/muted); citron only for active/advisory state. Library: Lucide.
  • The graphic device — "the spark": a single citron tick / upward stroke from "erupt." The "Zerupt is advising" marker, loading/thinking state, section accent. One per view, never a pattern.
  • Patterns/textures: avoid. Whitespace is the texture. No gradients or noise.

16Imagery & Art Direction

✓ Do

  • Real shop owners mid-work, hands on product, genuine expression, local MENA/India/SEA context.
  • Warm natural light, true color, shallow depth, slight warm grade to sit with cream.
  • Generous negative space for text overlay; subject off-center.

✕ Don't

  • Generic stock-tech, abstract 3D gradients, fake diverse boardrooms.
  • Screens glowing in dark rooms; anything cold or corporate.
  • Heavy filters or unrealistic color.

The owner is the hero; Zerupt is the quiet force behind them.

18Data Visualization

The data is the hero. Use the citron→olive→sage→ink ramp. Charts feel like insight handed to you, not a dashboard to decode — every chart implies a next action where possible. Numbers in Plex Mono; gridlines are faint ink hairlines; citron reserved for the single most important series or the recommended value.

20Touchpoints

  • Product UI: dark-first, cream/ink surfaces, citron only for focus/active/advising, ink CTAs, mono numbers.
  • Marketing site: quiet-luxury layout, owner-hero photography, confident-bold copy, one citron accent per view.
  • Social: outcome-led captions; show the product doing the work and giving the next move; real owners over stock.
  • Stationery: ink card + cream wordmark + mono details, or cream card + ink wordmark + one citron edge.
  • Deck: cream slides, ink type, one citron accent per slide, big mono numbers, lots of air.
  • Email / receipts: restrained, trustworthy, mono for figures, never noisy.
Part E — Governance

21Do / Don't Summary

✓ Always

  • One citron accent, ~10% of a view
  • Ink for primary CTAs
  • Sentence case, outcome-led copy
  • IBM Plex everywhere; mono for numbers
  • Logical CSS (ms/me/ps/pe)
  • Real owner photography, warm & true
  • Calm motion; whitespace as texture
  • Verify ar + en on every screen

✕ Never

  • Citron text on light; >1 accent color
  • Citron buttons with white text
  • Jargon (ERP, module, SKU); Title Case headlines
  • Mixing in Inter / Noto / a serif
  • Physical margin-left / right
  • Stock-tech, 3D gradients, cold corporate
  • Springy/overshoot motion; patterns/noise
  • Shipping LTR-only or English-only
