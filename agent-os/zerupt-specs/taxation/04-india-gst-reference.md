# India GST Reference (2026)

> Last updated: 2026-03-14
> Sources: CBIC, GST Council, ClearTax, PwC, PIB (Press Information Bureau)
> Major update: GST 2.0 (56th GST Council meeting, Sep 3, 2025) — new slab structure

---

## 1. GST Structure

India uses a **dual GST** model — tax is split between Central and State governments.

| Tax | When Applied | Collected By |
|-----|-------------|-------------|
| **CGST** (Central GST) | Intra-state supply | Central Government |
| **SGST** (State GST) | Intra-state supply | State Government |
| **IGST** (Integrated GST) | Inter-state supply, imports | Central Government (apportioned to destination state) |
| **UTGST** (Union Territory GST) | Intra-UT supply (replaces SGST) | UT administration |
| **Compensation Cess** | On top of GST for luxury/demerit goods | Central Government |

### Rate Split Rule
- **Intra-state**: Rate splits 50/50 → 18% GST = 9% CGST + 9% SGST
- **Inter-state**: Full rate as IGST → 18% IGST
- Determination: First 2 digits of supplier GSTIN vs recipient GSTIN (state code)

### UTGST States (use UTGST instead of SGST)
State codes: Chandigarh (04), Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu (26), Lakshadweep (31), Ladakh (38), Andaman & Nicobar (35), Puducherry (34)

## 2. GST 2.0 Rate Slabs (effective Sep 22, 2025)

The 56th GST Council meeting (Sep 3, 2025) rationalized slabs. CBIC notifications issued Sep 17, 2025.

### New Structure
| Slab | Purpose | Examples |
|------|---------|---------|
| **0%** | Essential/exempt | Fresh produce, milk, bread, salt, eggs, unprocessed cereals |
| **0.25%** | Niche | Rough diamonds, precious stones |
| **3%** | Niche | Gold, silver, platinum |
| **5%** | Essential processed | Packaged food, sugar, tea, coffee, edible oils, footwear <₹1000, apparel <₹1000, medicines, fertilizers, coal, transport services. **99% of former 12% items moved here.** |
| **18%** | Standard | Electronics, appliances (ACs, TVs, fridges, washing machines), cement, computers, cameras, soaps, shampoo, IT services, financial services, telecom. **90% of former 28% items moved here.** Small cars (<1200cc petrol, <1500cc diesel, <4m length). |
| **40%** | Sin/luxury | Pan masala, tobacco, aerated/caffeinated beverages, luxury cars (>4m or >engine thresholds), yachts, private aircraft |

### Key Migrations
| Item | Old Rate | New Rate |
|------|----------|----------|
| Butter, ghee, processed cheese | 12% | 5% |
| Fruit juices, namkeen | 12% | 5% |
| Apparel/footwear > ₹1000 | 12% | 5% |
| ACs, TVs, fridges, washing machines | 28% | 18% |
| Cement | 28% | 18% |
| Paints | 28% | 18% |
| Small cars | 28% | 18% |
| Aerated beverages | 28% | 40% |
| Pan masala, tobacco | 28% + cess | 40% |

## 4. Composition Scheme

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Turnover threshold (goods) | ₹1.5 crore |
| Turnover threshold (services) | ₹50 lakh |
| NE/special category states | ₹75 lakh |
| Goods traders rate | 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) |
| Manufacturers rate | 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) |
| Restaurants rate | 5% (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) |
| Service providers rate | 6% (3% CGST + 3% SGST) |

### Restrictions
- Cannot collect GST from customers (issue "bill of supply", not tax invoice)
- Cannot claim Input Tax Credit
- Cannot make inter-state supplies
- Cannot supply via e-commerce
- Excluded: ice cream, pan masala, tobacco manufacturers
- Filing: CMP-08 quarterly, GSTR-4 annual

## 5. Input Tax Credit (ITC)

### Utilization Order
1. IGST credit → offset IGST, then CGST, then SGST
2. CGST credit → offset CGST, then IGST (not SGST)
3. SGST credit → offset SGST, then IGST (not CGST)

### Blocked Credits (Section 17(5)) — NO ITC
- Motor vehicles (except for specified businesses)
- Food, beverages, outdoor catering, beauty, health (unless same line of business)
- Club memberships, fitness centers
- Rent-a-cab, life/health insurance (unless obligatory)
- Works contract for immovable property construction
- Personal consumption
- Goods lost, stolen, destroyed, written off, free samples
- Tax paid due to fraud (Section 74)

### ITC Reversal
- **Rule 42**: Proportional reversal for inputs used for both taxable and exempt
- **Rule 43**: Capital goods used partly for exempt
- **Section 16(4)**: Claim by earlier of: Sep return of next FY, or annual return filing

## 6. Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)

### Services Under RCM (Section 9(3))
| Service | Supplier | Recipient (pays GST) |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| GTA transport | Goods Transport Agency | Specified recipients |
| Legal services | Advocate/firm | Any business entity |
| Director services | Company director | Company |
| Insurance agent | Insurance agent | Insurance company |
| Recovery agent | Recovery agent | Bank/NBFC |
| Import of services | Foreign supplier | Indian recipient |
| Author/photographer | Individual | Publisher/media company |
| Security services | Non-body corporate | Registered person |
| Renting motor vehicle | Non-body corporate | Body corporate |

### Goods Under RCM
- Cashew nuts, bidi wrapper leaves, tobacco leaves, silk yarn, raw cotton
- Used vehicles, seized goods from government

### RCM Compliance
- Must register under GST regardless of turnover threshold
- Pay in cash (not through ITC) at time of filing
- Issue self-invoice if supplier is unregistered
- ITC available on RCM tax paid (subject to normal rules)

### 2025 Change
- Sponsorship services **removed** from RCM (Jan 16, 2025) — now under forward charge

## 7. E-Invoicing

### Turnover Thresholds (progressive lowering)
| Date | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Oct 2020 | ₹500 crore |
| Jan 2021 | ₹100 crore |
| Apr 2021 | ₹50 crore |
| Apr 2022 | ₹20 crore |
| Oct 2022 | ₹10 crore |
| Aug 2023 | **₹5 crore** (current as of 2026) |

### IRN (Invoice Reference Number)
- 64-character hash generated by Invoice Registration Portal (IRP)
- Unique identifier for every B2B invoice
- Generated by uploading invoice JSON to IRP (einvoice1.gst.gov.in)
- GSTN JSON schema v1.1

### QR Code
- Auto-generated by IRP for e-invoiced B2B invoices
- Mandatory on B2C invoices for turnover > ₹500 crore
- Contains: supplier GSTIN, recipient GSTIN, invoice number, date, value, line items, HSN (main), IRN

### Reporting Window
- Turnover ≥ ₹10 crore: **30-day window** — cannot generate e-invoice for bills older than 30 days
- Cancellation: within 24 hours on IRP

## 8. HSN / SAC Code Requirements

| Aggregate Turnover | HSN Digits Required |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| Up to ₹5 crore | 4-digit HSN |
| Above ₹5 crore | 6-digit HSN |
| Exports / Imports | 8-digit HSN |

- **SAC**: 6-digit codes for services (e.g., 9954 construction, 9971 financial, 9983 IT)
- HSN mandatory on all tax invoices
- GSTR-1 requires HSN summary table

## 10. ERP Data Model Implications

For Zerupt's tax configuration, India GST requires:

1. **State-aware tax determination**: Supplier GSTIN state code vs recipient GSTIN state code → intra-state (CGST+SGST) or inter-state (IGST)
2. **UTGST handling**: Detect union territory state codes and apply UTGST instead of SGST
3. **Compound cess**: Cess calculated on base amount (not on base + GST) — but modeled as compound in group
4. **HSN code per product**: Mandatory, digit count depends on tenant turnover
5. **E-invoice integration**: JSON generation, IRP API, IRN storage, QR code generation
6. **RCM flag per supplier/service**: Self-invoice generation, cash payment tracking
7. **ITC tracking**: Per-purchase eligibility, blocked credits, reversal rules
8. **Composition scheme flag**: Tenant-level, affects invoicing (no tax invoice, bill of supply only)
9. **Date-effective rates**: GST 2.0 transition (pre/post Sep 22, 2025), cess changes
