# Phase 2: Niching Down

> Source: $100M Offers, Chapter 7 — "Niching Down"

## Core Principle

**"The riches are in the niches."**

The goal is to be a big fish in a small pond, not a small fish in the ocean. When you niche down, you can:
- Charge MORE (specialists always out-earn generalists)
- Convert MORE (prospects feel "this was made for ME")
- Deliver BETTER results (you deeply understand the specific problem)
- Get better referrals (word of mouth is stronger in tight communities)

## The Niching Framework

A niche is defined by 3 dimensions:

### WHO (The specific person/company)
- Not "business owners" — WHICH business owners?
- Not "SaaS companies" — what KIND of SaaS? What stage? What vertical?
- Get specific: industry, company size, revenue range, role of buyer, geography, tech stack, business model

### WHAT (The specific problem you solve)
- Not "we help with growth" — WHICH aspect of growth?
- Not "we improve operations" — which SPECIFIC operational pain?
- The more specific the problem, the more valuable the solution feels

### HOW (Your unique mechanism/approach)
- What makes your approach different from every other solution?
- What is the proprietary process, framework, or technology?
- This is what makes you incomparable (not just "better")

## The Niche-Down Test

Keep niching until you can say: **"I help [SPECIFIC WHO] achieve [SPECIFIC RESULT] through [SPECIFIC MECHANISM] in [SPECIFIC TIMEFRAME]"**

If any part is vague, you haven't niched enough.

## Why Founders Resist Niching

Hormozi addresses the fear directly:
- "But I'll lose customers!" — No, you'll lose BAD customers and attract GREAT ones
- "My market will be too small!" — A small market you OWN beats a big market where you're invisible
- "I can help everyone!" — Maybe, but you can't MARKET to everyone. Niche to market, expand to serve.

## The Expansion Path

1. **Start narrow** — dominate one niche completely
2. **Expand the WHO** — same offer, adjacent audience
3. **Expand the WHAT** — same audience, adjacent problem
4. Never expand both at once

## Interview Goal

By the end of this phase, you should have:
- A specific WHO (not a broad category)
- A specific WHAT (the exact problem)
- A specific HOW (their unique mechanism)
- A one-sentence niche statement they'd be proud to put on their website
